Our mission

Keep every recipe you love. Cook it without the hunt.

Garde exists so the dish you made once and never forgot is always one tap away, wherever you found it.

Where we started

October 29, 2026

A problem in the kitchen

Jonny loves to cook, and he could never find the recipes he loved. They were scattered across bookmarks, screenshots, blog posts and scraps of paper. So he started building one calm place for all of them.

October 2026

The first version

The first build could save a recipe from a link or a photo of a recipe card and keep it in one place. It was called Recolio, then Commis, until the name finally clicked.

November 2026

Garde

The name comes from garde manger, the kitchen station that keeps and prepares the pantry for everyone else. Around it grew the Pantry shopping list, Sous weekly planning, Garde Reserve and a support team.

Why we build it

Your recipes are yours

They're private to you, and you can export all of them whenever you like.

Fast beats fancy

Saving a recipe should take seconds, not an evening of copying and pasting.

Cooking should feel calm

No story to scroll past, no ads between the steps. Just the ingredients and what to do.

About me

Hi, I'm Jonny.

I'm the person behind Garde. I love cooking, and I love building things for the web.

Garde started as a fix for my own problem: I'd make something great, forget where I found it, and spend an hour looking for it. I wanted a place that felt as good to open as the meal felt to eat.

I design and build every part of it myself, from the Pantry list to the support desk. If something's confusing or broken, open a ticket and it comes straight to me and the team.
