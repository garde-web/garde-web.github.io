GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions.

What is Garde Reserve?

Garde Reserve is the premium membership for Garde. It provides unlimited recipe storage, access to Sous, priority support, and exclusive account recognition.

How much does Reserve cost?

Reserve is priced at $9.99 per month.

How do I become a member?

Sign into your Garde account and submit a Reserve access request. Our team will help you through the setup process in your support ticket.

What happens to my recipes if I upgrade?

Your existing recipe collection stays yours. Reserve simply removes the standard recipe storage limit so your collection can continue growing.

What is Sous?

Sous is Garde's meal planning experience. It lets you organize meals using recipes from your collection and send ingredients to your Pantry.

Do Reserve members get faster support?

Yes. Reserve support tickets receive priority placement in the support queue.

Is Reserve available immediately?

Reserve is rolling out through access requests. Once your request has been reviewed, our team will assist with getting your membership set up.
