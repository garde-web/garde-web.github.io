Good to know

How much does it cost?

$9.99 a month. Online billing is still being set up, so for now we activate Reserve for you directly after you request access, and we'll tell you before any charge starts.

What happens to my recipes if Reserve ends?

Everything you saved stays yours. You keep your recipes, photos and past plans. You just can't add recipes beyond the free limit of {{limit}}, and the Sous planner, What can I cook? and smart scanning pause until you're back.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Ask in a support ticket and we'll end it. There's no long-term commitment.

Where do Sous recipes come from?

They're written and tested by us. When you add one to your plan, a copy is saved to your own collection so you can edit it, scale it and cook from it like any other recipe.

{{#if smart}}
What happens to photos I scan with smart scan?

The photo is sent securely to Google's Gemini service just to read the recipe, and isn't kept by Garde unless you choose to keep the scanned card with your recipe. See the Privacy Policy.

{{/if}}
Is it worth it if I only cook now and then?

Probably not yet. The free plan has everything you need to save, scale and cook from up to {{limit}} recipes. Reserve is for people planning their weeks or building a big collection.
