Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Garde is a recipe-keeping website run by Jonny. This page explains what information Garde collects, why, and the choices you have. You can reach us any time through the Support page.

What we collect

  • Your Google account details: your name, email address and profile picture, which Google shares with us when you sign in.
  • What you save: recipes (titles, ingredients, steps, notes, tags, links, and any photos you upload or scanned cards you choose to keep), your meal plans and templates, and recipes you choose to share.
  • Support: tickets and messages you send us, plus your browser and device description so we can help.
  • Technical reports: if something breaks while you're signed in, your browser sends us the error message, the page you were on and your browser type. We also record when you last visited.

How we use it

To run Garde: show you your recipes, build your lists and plans, answer your support tickets, grant and manage Garde Reserve, and fix problems. We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.

Where it's kept, and who else sees it

  • Your data is stored with Google Firebase (sign-in and database). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Link import: the web address you import is sent to a service that fetches the page for your browser, which may be our own helper or public ones such as corsproxy.io, allorigins.win, codetabs.com and r.jina.ai.
  • Photo scan: basic scanning happens inside your browser. The photo is not uploaded.
  • Smart scan (Garde Reserve): the photo is sent to our helper and then to Google's Gemini service, only to read the recipe. We don't keep it unless you choose to keep the scanned card.
  • Fonts and a text-recognition tool load from Google Fonts and jsDelivr.
  • Only our staff can see support tickets and basic account details such as your name, email and Reserve status. Staff cannot read your recipes.

Sharing

Your recipes are private unless you press Share. A shared recipe is visible to anyone who has its link until you stop sharing it from the recipe page.

Your choices

  • Export: download all your recipes from Settings.
  • Delete: Settings has a Delete account button that permanently removes your account, recipes, photos, plans, templates, shared links and profile. Support tickets you've sent are kept so we can finish helping you, and can be removed if you ask us.
  • Questions or requests about your data: open a ticket on the Support page.

Local storage

Your browser keeps small settings on your device, such as units, servings and which Pantry items you've ticked, plus your sign-in session. These stay on your device.

Children

Garde isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, post a notice on the site.
