Features

Link import

Pull a recipe from a web page into your collection.

Photo scan

Read text from recipe cards and cookbook pages.

Search

Find recipes by name, ingredient or tag.

Favorites

Pin the recipes you make again and again.

Collections

See recipes grouped by tag.

Pantry

One shopping list, combined, scaled and sorted by aisle.

Scale servings

Change servings and every amount adapts.

US and metric

Switch a recipe between cups and grams, or °F and °C.

Cook mode

Big, one-step-at-a-time view with timers, and the screen stays awake.

Your own photos

Add a photo of the dish and keep the scanned recipe card.

Made it and ratings

Track what you cook, how often, and how much you liked it.

Share and print

Send a recipe with a link, or print a clean copy.

Install the app

Add Garde to your phone's home screen.

Save from your phone

Always in my kitchen

Tell Garde what you always have and it stays off your shopping list.

Duplicate check

Garde warns you if you already saved a recipe.

Editing and notes

Fix amounts and add notes any time.

Export

Private
