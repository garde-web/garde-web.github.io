Features

Everything Garde does to keep your recipes organized.

Link import

Pull a recipe from a web page into your collection.

Photo scan

Read text from recipe cards and cookbook pages.

Search

Find recipes by name, ingredient or tag.

Favorites

Pin your favorite recipes for quick access.

Collections

View recipes in the similar categories.

Pantry

A shopping list that includes all the ingredients in your recipes.

Adaptive servings

Change serving sizes to change the measurements accordingly.

Measurement Conversion

Convert a recipe written in metric to US, and vice versa.

Cook mode

Big, step-by-step view. Automatically keeps the screen awake as well so that is pretty cool.

Your own photos

Add a photo of the dish and keep the scanned recipe card.

Made it and ratings

Track what you cook, how often, and how much you liked it.

Share and print

Send a recipe with a link, or print a clean copy.

Install the app

Add Garde to your phone's home screen.

Save from your phone

Share a recipe page to Garde from your Android browser and it imports itself.

Always in my kitchen

Tell Garde what you always have and it stays off your shopping list.

Duplicate check

Garde warns you if you already saved a recipe.

Editing and notes

Fix amounts and add notes any time.

Export

Download all your recipes as a file.

Private

Your recipes are visible only to you unless you share one.

Support tickets

Ask for help and follow the reply in one place.

Garde Reserve

Unlimited recipes, Sous planner, What can I cook?, smart scanning and priority support.
