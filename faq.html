Is Garde free?

Yes. Free accounts can save up to 50 recipes. Garde Reserve ($9.99/month) removes the limit and adds the Sous meal planner and priority support.

Who can see my recipes?

Only you. Each account's recipes are private and tied to your Google sign-in.

Does link import work on every site?

It works on most sites. For the few that block automatic import, the Save to Garde bookmarklet on the Add page grabs the recipe straight from your browser.

How does the Pantry work?

Pick recipes, set how many servings you want, and Garde combines and scales every ingredient into one shopping list sorted by aisle.

What is Sous?

Sous is the weekly meal planner in Garde Reserve. Plan breakfast, lunch and dinner for the week, and the ingredients flow into your Pantry.

Can I save recipes from my phone?

Yes. Install Garde from Settings. On Android you can then share any recipe page to Garde from your browser's Share menu and it imports automatically. On iPhone, paste the link on the Add page, or use the Save to Garde bookmark in Safari.

Can I stop things like salt showing up on my shopping list?

Yes. On the Pantry page, press "Have it" next to any item, or add your basics under "Always in my kitchen". Those items are left off your lists from then on.

Can I use handwritten recipe cards?

Yes. Upload a photo and Garde reads the text. Clear, well-lit printed text works best; handwriting may need some cleanup.

How do I get help?

Open a ticket from the Support page. A member of the Garde team will reply right in your ticket.

Can I take my recipes with me?

Yes. Settings has an export button that downloads everything as a file.
